abCheck helps you understand the hardware, system and battery information exposed by your Android device.

View battery level, charge status, temperature, voltage, live current, charging power, battery technology, design capacity, full-charge capacity, health status, and cycle count in one Material 3 dashboard.

Battery reporting varies between Android versions and manufacturers. abCheck prefers public Android APIs, uses narrow local fallbacks when available, and clearly marks estimated cycle values. When the device does not expose enough information, the app shows N/A instead of inventing a measurement.

Features:
* Live charging and discharging current chart
* Reported or best-effort battery cycle count
* Design and full-charge capacity when exposed by the device
* Light, dark, and system themes
* 16 languages
* No root required
* No ads or analytics
* Hardware, system, network, apps, camera and live sensor information
* Display, touch, key, audio, microphone and camera tests
* Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, GPS and USB tools
* Local CPU, memory, storage and GPU benchmarks
* Foreground battery recording, floating monitors and widgets
* Export device reports as Text, JSON, HTML, PDF or PNG
* No account or automatic telemetry
* Optional permissions requested by the tools that need them

abCheck is free and open-source software licensed under Apache-2.0.

Network access is used by connectivity tools that you start. All diagnostic history remains local unless you explicitly export or share it. New diagnostic labels are available in English and Vietnamese; existing battery translations remain available in 16 languages. Root and Shizuku are optional for advanced read-only diagnostics.
