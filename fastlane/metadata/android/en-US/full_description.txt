Get to know your Android device with abCheck Device & System. Explore hardware details, check components and follow live system readings in one free, open-source app.

A clearer way to explore your phone
The redesigned dashboard puts battery, memory, storage and CPU status together. Four main destinations - Dashboard, Device, Tests and Tools - make it easy to find information and return to your checks. Choose light, dark or system appearance.

Device information
Explore processor, graphics, display, memory, Android build, network, installed apps, camera capabilities and live sensors. Search supported information pages and filter visible apps.

Hardware checks
Run display, brightness, multitouch, button, vibration, speaker, earpiece, microphone, flashlight, camera and sensor checks. Record your own Pass or Fail observations. Available checks depend on your device and permissions.

Battery insights
See level, charging state, temperature, voltage, current and power. View health, capacity and cycle information when Android exposes them. Follow the current chart and record battery sessions locally.

Tools and live monitoring
Use Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, GPS, USB and user-started network diagnostics. Explore system graphs, floating monitors, home-screen widgets, local benchmarks and saved history. Export device reports as Text, JSON, HTML, PDF or PNG.

Your data stays under your control
No ads, analytics or account required. Diagnostic history is stored locally. Connectivity tools use network access when you start them. Reports are shared only when you choose to export or share. Optional permissions are requested by the tools that need them. Advanced read-only root and Shizuku diagnostics require separate setup.

Understand the limits
Android and manufacturers restrict some readings. Unavailable values remain N/A and estimates are labeled. abCheck does not reconstruct battery history from before installation. Local benchmark scores describe abCheck workloads and are not comparable to other benchmark apps. Core diagnostics work without root.

Diagnostic labels are available in English and Vietnamese, with English fallbacks where needed. Existing battery screens retain translations in 16 languages.
